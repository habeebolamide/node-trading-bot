# Strategy platform

## Platform and stack

Web operator dashboard: React/Vite, Express/TypeScript, PostgreSQL/Drizzle, Node 22.

## Product truth

The PRD in docs/prd.md governs implementation. An owner configures and operates independent research bots in a shared paper portfolio. Viewers inspect the same account without mutation rights. Production LIVE trading remains disabled. Money in the dashboard is simulated and uses authoritative ledger projections. Pausing or stopping does not prove exposure has closed. Telegram is one-way, asynchronous, and independent of trading.

## Confirmed direction

The user approved a calm, light workspace named “Strategy platform”, with a portfolio table, focused bot detail views, and prominent unresolved-exposure warnings. Success is understanding capital, lifecycle, decisions and faults, and submitting authorized durable controls without confusing acceptance with completion.
